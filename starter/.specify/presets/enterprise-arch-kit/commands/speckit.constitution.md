## Enterprise Rules for the Constitution (read first)

This project uses the enterprise architecture kit. The constitution is
derived from the enterprise standards, not authored by the builder.

- Start from the resolved `constitution-template` (the enterprise
  constitution) and the `enterprise-standards` skill. Keep principles I–VII
  and the gates intact.
- Fill project-specific values (project name, ratification date) from the
  repository and today's date. **Don't ask the builder for principles,
  technology or governance rules.**
- If the builder supplies extra rules, add them as project-specific
  amendments only if they don't weaken an enterprise standard; otherwise
  record them in `docs/production-handoff.md` §7 as a preference and keep the
  standard.
- Any question you genuinely need answered must be a plain business question
  from the `business-context` skill.
