# Welcome — build what you need with AI

You describe what you want in everyday words. The AI builds it, asks a few
simple questions along the way, and saves your work as it goes. You don't
need to know how to code.

Behind the scenes it follows the company's technology standards, so if your
idea takes off, the technology team can take it live without starting over.

## Get started (about 5 minutes, once)

1. **Make your own copy.** At the top of this page on GitHub, click
   **Use this template → Create a new repository**. Give it a name like
   `team-leave-tracker` and click **Create repository**.
2. **Open it in your browser.** In your new repository, click the green
   **Code** button → **Codespaces** → **Create codespace on main**. Wait a few
   minutes the first time while everything gets ready. Nothing to install on
   your computer.
3. **Start the AI.** At the bottom of the screen there's a panel called
   **Terminal**. Click into it and type one of these, then press Enter:
   - `claude` — for Claude Code
   - `codex` — for Codex

   The first time, it will ask you to sign in. Follow the steps on screen.
4. **Just ask.** Describe what you'd like, for example:
   - "Make a simple app where my team can request leave and I can approve it."
   - "Build a dashboard of how many support tickets we get each week."
   - "Create a form for new joiners to submit their details."

## Good to know

- **Seeing your app:** when the AI starts your app, a preview opens
  automatically (or look for the **Ports** tab and click the globe icon).
- **It saves as it goes.** Ask "back this up to GitHub" whenever you want
  your work stored online.
- **Changed your mind?** Just say so — "make the button green", "undo that",
  "add a way to export to Excel".
- **Something not working?** Describe what you see, in your own words.
- **Ready for others to use it?** Ask "Can this go live?" The AI will check
  a few things with you and prepare everything the technology team needs.

## Rules that keep everyone safe

- Use made-up example data while building — never real customer or staff
  records.
- Don't paste passwords or secret keys into the chat or the project.
- If you're unsure whether something is allowed, ask the AI — it knows the
  company's rules.
